=== district | owner of PEDAL POINT
PIS:
- Explain marketing and its importance in a global economy
- Describe marketing functions and related activities
- Explain the concept of competition
- Identify factors affecting a business's profit
SITUATION:
You are to assume the role of a sales associate at PEDAL POINT, a bicycle shop in a mid-sized college town. The owner (judge) wants to know what marketing is and why the shop should spend time and money on it.

PEDAL POINT sells commuter bikes, mountain bikes and repair services. The owner has always believed that a good shop will sell itself, and has never planned any marketing. Last year a national chain opened a store 10 minutes away and began offering low prices on similar bikes. Since then, the shop's profit has dropped by 18 percent, and the owner is unsure what to change.

The owner (judge) does not understand what marketing includes beyond advertising. You must explain the main marketing functions and how they could help the shop compete and protect its profit.

You will explain your ideas to the owner (judge) in a role-play to take place at the shop. The owner (judge) will begin the role-play by greeting you and asking about marketing. After you have explained your ideas and have answered the owner's (judge's) questions, the owner (judge) will conclude the role-play by thanking you for your work.
QUESTIONS:
- Which marketing function should the shop work on first?
- How can the shop compete without lowering all of its prices?
HOOK: A bike shop's profit fell 18 percent after a national chain moved in.
