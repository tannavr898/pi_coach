=== district | branch manager of LAKEVIEW CREDIT UNION
PIS:
- Describe functions of money (medium of exchange, unit of measure, store of value)
- Explain forms of financial exchange (cash, credit, debit, electronic funds transfer, etc.)
- Explain the need to save and invest
- Describe types of financial-services providers
SITUATION:
You are to assume the role of a member service representative at LAKEVIEW CREDIT UNION, a not-for-profit financial institution serving a small city. The branch manager (judge) wants you to explain money and payment choices to a group of high school students who will visit the branch next week.

LAKEVIEW CREDIT UNION offers checking and savings accounts, debit cards and small loans. Most of the visiting students have part-time jobs and are paid by direct deposit or by check, but many carry cash and have never used a savings account. The branch has 30 minutes with the group.

The branch manager (judge) wants to know what you will say about what money does, the different ways people pay for things, and why saving matters. You should also explain how a credit union differs from other places to keep money.

You will explain your ideas to the branch manager (judge) in a role-play to take place in the branch office. The branch manager (judge) will begin the role-play by greeting you and asking about the student visit. After you have explained your ideas and have answered the branch manager's (judge's) questions, the branch manager (judge) will conclude the role-play by thanking you for your work.
QUESTIONS:
- Why might a student choose a debit card over cash?
- What is one reason a student should open a savings account now?
HOOK: A credit union has 30 minutes to teach a room of teenagers how money works.
