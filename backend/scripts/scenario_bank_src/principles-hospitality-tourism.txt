=== district | general manager of SEASIDE INN
PIS:
- Explain the principles of supply and demand
- Describe the functions of prices in markets
- Identify factors affecting a business's profit
- Explain the concept of competition
SITUATION:
You are to assume the role of a front desk agent at SEASIDE INN, a 60-room hotel near a popular beach. The general manager (judge) wants you to explain why room rates change from season to season and from weekday to weekend.

SEASIDE INN charges $240 a night in July and $95 a night in February. Last month, a guest complained that the same room cost $60 more on a Saturday than on a Tuesday. A new 40-room hotel opened a mile away and lists its rooms at $15 below SEASIDE INN's rate.

The general manager (judge) wants you to explain how demand, competition and costs affect the prices a hotel sets, and how the inn can protect its profit throughout the year.

You will explain your ideas to the general manager (judge) in a role-play to take place in the manager's office. The general manager (judge) will begin the role-play by greeting you and asking about room rates. After you have explained your ideas and have answered the general manager's (judge's) questions, the general manager (judge) will conclude the role-play by thanking you for your work.
QUESTIONS:
- What could the inn do in February to fill more rooms?
- How should staff explain price differences to guests who ask?
HOOK: The same beach hotel room costs $240 in July and $95 in February.
