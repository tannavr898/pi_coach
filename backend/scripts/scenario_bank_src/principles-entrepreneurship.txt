=== district | owner of GREEN THUMB GARDEN SHOP
PIS:
- Identify the impact of small business/entrepreneurship on market economies
- Describe types of business activities
- Explain the concept of private enterprise
- Identify factors affecting a business's profit
SITUATION:
You are to assume the role of a part-time employee at GREEN THUMB GARDEN SHOP, a small business that sells plants, soil and gardening tools. The owner (judge) wants you to explain what makes a small business important to the local economy.

GREEN THUMB GARDEN SHOP is owned by one person and employs six people, mostly students. The owner buys many plants from nearby growers and pays about $18,000 a year in local taxes. A home improvement chain recently began selling plants and tools at lower prices, and the owner is worried about whether the shop can stay profitable.

The owner (judge) asks you to explain the types of activities a business carries out, how a small business like this helps the local economy and what affects the shop's profit.

You will explain your ideas to the owner (judge) in a role-play to take place at the shop. The owner (judge) will begin the role-play by greeting you and asking about the shop's role in town. After you have explained your ideas and have answered the owner's (judge's) questions, the owner (judge) will conclude the role-play by thanking you for your work.
QUESTIONS:
- What does the shop provide to the community besides products?
- What is one way the shop could protect its profit against the chain?
HOOK: A garden shop that pays $18,000 in local taxes is undercut by a home improvement chain.
