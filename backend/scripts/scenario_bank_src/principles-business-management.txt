=== district | human resources manager of LAKESHORE TRANSIT
PIS:
- Discuss the nature of human resources management
- Orient new employees
- Follow chain of command
- Explain the rights of workers
SITUATION:
You are to assume the role of an employee in the human resources department at LAKESHORE TRANSIT, a city bus system with 240 drivers and 40 office staff. The human resources manager (judge) wants you to explain how the department helps new drivers get started and what rights they have.

LAKESHORE TRANSIT hired 35 new drivers this spring. Several of them said they did not know whom to report problems to, and two did not know that they were entitled to rest breaks. One new driver went straight to the general manager with a schedule complaint instead of speaking with a supervisor first.

The human resources manager (judge) asks you to explain what human resources management does, what an orientation for new drivers should include, why the chain of command matters and which rights workers have.

You will explain your ideas to the human resources manager (judge) in a role-play to take place at the transit office. The human resources manager (judge) will begin the role-play by greeting you and asking about the new drivers. After you have explained your ideas and have answered the human resources manager's (judge's) questions, the human resources manager (judge) will conclude the role-play by thanking you for your work.
QUESTIONS:
- Who should a new driver speak with first about a schedule problem?
- Why is it important for workers to know their rights from the first day?
HOOK: Thirty-five new bus drivers started this spring and many did not know who to ask for help.
