# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

DECA competitors — high-school and college business students preparing for role-play events — practicing on their own time, usually in the evenings or in the run-up to a competition. Their job is to rehearse a full role-play end to end: read an original scenario, prep against a real timer, deliver a response typed or spoken, field the judge's follow-ups, and study honest feedback on what they demonstrated. They arrive anxious about a high-stakes performance and leave when they feel ready for it.

The everyday surface is the trainer app, so PRODUCT.md defaults to the product register. The landing page and the no-login `/demo` walkthrough are brand-register surfaces of equal importance: when a task targets those, treat them as `brand` (design is the product there) rather than inheriting the product default.

## Product Purpose

PI Coach is an AI trainer that runs a DECA role-play as a complete rep: interpret a free-text practice request, generate an original scenario built to require a handful of business skills, time the prep, capture a typed or spoken response, ask judge-style follow-ups, and return coaching feedback on both content and delivery. It grades the substance judges reward using its own independently authored 282-criterion framework, not DECA's licensed performance-indicator list. Success is a student who walks into the real thing feeling ready — reps exist to convert competition-day nerves into confidence.

## Positioning

Practice coaching, never a judge score: PI Coach gives a full, original role-play rep with honest, evidence-backed feedback on the skills a student actually demonstrated — the only place to rehearse the whole loop, scenario through delivery, on an evaluation framework that mirrors what judges reward without copying it.

## Brand Personality

Supportive, sharp, modern. The voice is a coach in the student's corner: it encourages without coddling and stays specific enough to be believed. Feedback lands as help, not a verdict — the honesty of "coaching, never a score" is the trust it trades on. Contemporary and polished, with a competitive edge that respects the stakes without manufacturing pressure.

## Anti-references

Not gamified or cartoonish — no mascots, confetti, badge-grinding, or playful blobs; the stakes are real and the tone should respect that. Not clinical exam software — none of the sterile gray, standardized-test, proctoring-tool coldness that would make practice feel like the pressure it's meant to relieve. The design lives deliberately between those two poles: serious without being cold, encouraging without being childish.

## Design Principles

Coaching, not judging — every screen frames output as practice help that moves a student forward, never a verdict passed on them.

Readiness is the metric — the north star is a student who feels prepared, so each surface should visibly close the gap between "nervous" and "ready" rather than just display data.

Honest and specific — trust is earned by precise, evidence-backed feedback tied to what the student actually said; vague praise and inflated scores erode the whole premise.

Practice like the real thing — original scenarios, a real timer, and real voice delivery keep fidelity to competition high, while the independent framework keeps the IP the product's own.

Hold the line between the poles — resist both the gamified-cartoon reflex and the clinical-exam reflex on every decision; when a choice drifts toward either, correct back to serious-but-encouraging.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA: body text at ≥4.5:1 contrast, fully keyboard-navigable flows, and properly labeled controls throughout — including the timed prep and voice-capture steps, which must not depend on a mouse or on hearing alone. Reduced-motion is already honored in code (the loader keeps a slow breathing pulse so it never reads as hung); preserve that carve-out. Voice is always paired with a typed path so no step requires speech.
